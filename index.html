How it works

From screenshot to reminder in seconds

Screenshots are how we save things for later. Then later never comes, and they sink into your camera roll.

  1. 1

    Screenshot it

    Take a screenshot as usual, then tap Share.

  2. 2

    Choose SnapRecaller

    Pick it from the share sheet. SnapRecaller only sees the one image you share with it.

  3. 3

    Type when and why

    One line, like “tomorrow 9am send Priya the deck”, or just the time. Tap Save. At that time, a notification arrives with the screenshot attached.

One line, no pickers

Type the time and the note together. SnapRecaller splits them apart as you type, so you can adjust either one before saving, or tap a quick option instead. The options you use most move to the front.

tomorrow 9am send Priya the deck
Remind
Tomorrow, 9:00 AM
Note
send Priya the deck
  • in 3 hours
  • tomorrow morning
  • this weekend
  • next week

Just say when. It writes the note.

Type only the time and SnapRecaller reads the screenshot and suggests a short note. Keep it, tweak it, or write your own. It’s done by Apple’s on-device language model, so nothing is uploaded to be read.

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, you get a simpler suggestion taken from the screenshot’s text.

sun 4pm
Remind
Sunday, 4:00 PM
Note AI
Charred Corn & Miso Butter Pasta

In the app

Upcoming at the top, soonest first

  • Swipe to keep it moving

    Swipe right to mark a reminder done. Swipe left to reschedule or delete, or long-press for the same options. Changed your mind? Undo is one tap away.

  • Tap to see it again

    Open any reminder to see the full screenshot and its note. Edit the note or mark it done right there.

  • Not sure when?

    Save a screenshot without a time and it’s kept with its note, ready when you are.

Privacy

Stays on your iPhone

SnapRecaller has no servers and no networking code. What you save is stored on your iPhone, not on a server.

  • No account, no sign-upOpen it and start saving. There’s nothing to log in to.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackingNothing about how you use the app is collected or shared.
  • Never sent to us or anyone elseSuggested notes are written on your iPhone, and reminders are local notifications.
  • Only the screenshot you shareSnapRecaller never asks for access to your photo library.
